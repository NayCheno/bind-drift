\section{Evaluation Protocol and Results}

The evaluation is organized around five research questions. Each RQ is written
as a closed loop: question, method, data, result, interpretation, and threat.

\begin{description}
\item[RQ1] Can \tool{} extract reliable cross-language drift facts?
\item[RQ2] Does evidence gating reduce review volume while preserving useful
review targets?
\item[RQ3] Does \primary{} improve Top-K review yield over strong baselines?
\item[RQ4] What semantic drift patterns appear in adjudicated cases?
\item[RQ5] How reproducible is the artifact across versioned toolchains?
\end{description}

The main experiment replays 20 adjacent Linux mainline pairs across 21
snapshots. The canonical replay records 16,757 drift facts and 320 promoted
Rust-impact warnings. Paper numbers are generated from artifact outputs and
indexed with sha256 provenance.

\input{tables/rq1-extractor-audit}

For RQ1, the strict extractor audit covers 830 facts, including 150 promoted
warning evidence chains, and reports Cohen's kappa = 1.0. This supports use of
the extracted evidence for ranking and tables, but not completeness claims.

\input{tables/rq2-warning-volume}

For RQ2, evidence gating reduces 16,757 drift facts to 320 promoted warnings, a
98.09\% reduction. Fixed review budgets then inspect 10, 20, 50, or 100 ranked
warnings rather than the full drift stream.

\input{tables/rq3-ranking}

For RQ3, \primary{} is compared with simple baselines and ablations on the same
800-row pooled review set. Build-breakage and wrapper-fix oracles define labels
and validation checks only. Reviewer roles do not receive ranker names, ranks,
scores, adjudicated labels, or the other reviewer's notes before producing
independent labels.

\input{tables/rq4-semantic-review}

For RQ4, the semantic review pass separates \truesemantic{} from
\truewrapper{}. The semantic result is reported as review-target evidence, not
as confirmed runtime defects. False-positive risk is reported as a taxonomy
rather than overall warning-set precision. The pass samples 400 semantic target
candidates and reviews 360 adjudicated semantic rows.

\input{tables/rq5-reproducibility}

For RQ5, the reproduction gate records run manifests, toolchain matrices,
strict gates, and table sha256 provenance. The arm64 external-validity slice
replays 8 release tags, completes 7 adjacent pairs, records 7,086 drift facts,
promotes 248 warnings, and reports warning overlap and warning-type deltas
against the x86\_64 run. Failed pairs would be recorded with pair status, build
status, and error text.
